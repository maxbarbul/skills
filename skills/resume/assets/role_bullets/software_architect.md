Software Architect — Bullet Library

Guidance:
- Use these architecture-focused templates when you need to demonstrate system-level thinking, trade-offs, and concrete outcomes.

Architecture & Design
- Led architecture redesign for {system}, selecting {pattern} and driving a migration that improved scalability to {X} requests/sec and reduced cost by {Y}%.
- Introduced contract-first APIs and versioning strategy, enabling {N} teams to integrate without coordination overhead and reducing breaking changes by {X}%.
- Defined non-functional requirements (SLOs, capacity planning) and enforced them via automated tests and pipelines, keeping SLI compliance above {X}%.

Governance & Standards
- Established architecture review board and guidelines (anti-patterns, ownership model), which sped up reviews and reduced tech-debt growth.
- Created reusable platform components (auth, telemetry, deployments) that standardized services and reduced build time for new services by {X}%.

Proofs & Migration
- Piloted a phased migration plan with canary deployments and feature flags, preventing regressions and maintaining >{X}% availability during rollout.
- Performed capacity planning and load testing that informed a cloud rightsizing initiative, saving ${X}K/year.

Communication & Leadership
- Aligned engineering, product, and security stakeholders on the architecture vision; drove consensus and measurable adoption across {N} teams.
- Wrote architecture docs and runbooks to institutionalize knowledge; decreased onboarding time by {X}%.

How to tailor:
- Emphasize scope (number of systems, teams, users) and the measurable impact of architectural choices.
- Avoid vague phrases—state the chosen trade-offs and why.

---

Questionnaire prompts (use when role = "architect")

- Exact job title and dates.  
- Systems/teams impacted and level of ownership (design authority, reviewer, executor).  
- What were the main non-functional goals (SLOs, capacity, reliability, cost) and how were they measured?  
- Describe a representative architecture decision you led: problem, options considered, chosen trade-off, and outcome (metrics, incidents avoided).  
- Migration or rollout strategies used (canaries, feature flags, phased migration) and availability during rollout.  

How to use these prompts:
- Use these to capture technical trade-offs and measurable outcomes; convert answers into architecture-focused achievement bullets.
