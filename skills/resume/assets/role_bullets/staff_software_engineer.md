Staff Software Engineer — Bullet Library

Guidance:
- Use these templates as starting points. Replace placeholders (e.g., {X%}, {N}, {service}) with conservative, verified numbers.
- Aim for 3–6 high-impact bullets per recent role; prioritize measurable outcomes and scale.

Leadership & Strategy
- Led a cross-functional initiative to consolidate {N} services into a unified platform, reducing operational overhead by {X%} and shortening feature delivery time by {Y} weeks.
- Defined the technical roadmap and migration plan for {system}, resulting in {X}% improvement in developer productivity and {Y}% reduction in incidents.
- Mentored and grew a team of {N} engineers, establishing career ladders and quarterly growth plans; promoted {M} engineers.

System Design & Architecture
- Designed and rolled out a distributed event-driven architecture for {product}, improving throughput by {X}x and reducing end-to-end latency by {Y}%.
- Architected a multi-region failover strategy that achieved RTO < {T} and improved system availability to {X}%. 
- Introduced domain-driven design and bounded contexts, which reduced cross-team coupling and decreased release conflicts by {X}%.

Delivery & Impact
- Owned critical roadmap items delivering ${X}M in ARR through feature launches and performance optimizations.
- Led a major refactor that removed {N} legacy components, reducing tech debt and cutting release cycle time by {X}%. 
- Negotiated and led migration from monolith to microservices, enabling independent deploys and decreasing CI pipeline time by {X}%.

Reliability, Performance & Ops
- Implemented observability improvements (tracing, SLOs, dashboards) that reduced incident detection time by {X}% and MTTR by {Y}%.
- Optimized service autoscaling and resource configs to save {X}% on cloud costs while maintaining SLOs.

Developer Experience & Tooling
- Built internal platform/tooling (CI templates, SDKs, dev CLI) that cut onboarding time for new engineers from {T1} to {T2} days.
- Introduced contract testing and CI gates to prevent regressions, lowering production rollbacks by {X}%.

Cross-functional & Stakeholder
- Partnered with Product and Design to prioritize roadmap items, increasing feature usage by {X}% within {Y} months.
- Presented architecture and trade-offs to execs; secured $ {X} funding for major platform investment.

How to tailor:
- Prefer conservative, verifiable numbers. If unsure, flag as estimate for later verification.
- Keep bullets concise: Action (what) + Scope/Context (where) + Result (metric).

Questionnaire prompts (use when role = "staff")

- Exact job title, level, and dates.  
- Team(s) and organization size; reporting line and scope of ownership.  
- Describe the largest system or platform you influenced and your concrete role in decisions.  
- For each initiative: what was the problem, what did you design/build/lead, and what measurable impact occurred (uptime, MTTR, dev productivity, cost, revenue)?  
- Mentoring, org/process changes, or cross-team programs you led—outcomes and scale.  

How to use these prompts:
- Use these to gather high-level impact statements and map them to the leadership and architecture bullets above. Ensure metrics are conservative and verifiable.
